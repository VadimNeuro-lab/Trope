This is a token-level operator. It does not modify R: R' = R exactly. The
only thing it changes is the decoding rule -- here top-H sampling, which at
each step sorts the next-token distribution, keeps the longest prefix whose
entropy stays under a budget of H bits, renormalises it and samples. Do not
edit the representation and do not report a structural change.

Radicality for this operator is the entropy budget in bits:

  H = clip(bits_per_rho * rho, min_bits, max_bits)

with bits_per_rho = 1.0, min_bits = 0.15 and max_bits = 6.0 from the
token_operators block of configs/decoding.yaml. Unlike the other two
token-level operators this one does not pre-clamp rho at rho_max
(sampling.rho_max, default 8.0): the clip at max_bits is what bounds it, and
clamping first would additionally flatten the budget over a range where
bits_per_rho still keeps it under max_bits. Larger rho admits a wider set of
continuations, so here radicality does run in the familiar direction.

Worked example. rho = 0.9 gives H = 0.9 bits: at a step where the top token
already carries most of the mass, the kept set is one or two tokens; at a
genuinely uncertain step it is wider. rho = 0.05 gives H = 0.15 bits, the
floor, and rho = 12 gives H = 6.0 bits, the ceiling.

Return one JSON object and nothing else:

{"entropy_budget": 0.9}

followed by a line beginning "change log:" naming the budget in bits and the
rho it came from.
