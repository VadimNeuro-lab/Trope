Specification. Negate an axiom of the categorical frame F, not an assumption
the parser happened to write down. The axioms of each frame and the
dependencies between them are declared in assets/frames.json; together they
form a DAG, and an axiom's depth is the number of axioms it transitively
rests on -- how far up the DAG it sits, not how much sits on it.

  Ranking. Order the axioms of F by depth, shallowest first, ties broken by
  name. Radicality indexes into that ranking:

    rho_hat = min(rho, rho_max) / rho_max,  rho_max is sampling.rho_max, default 8.0
    i       = min(n - 1, floor(rho_hat * n))   over the n axioms of F

  This is what "larger rho targets axioms higher in the dependency DAG"
  means: a small rho denies a free-standing axiom that rests on nothing else,
  a large rho denies the most derived one, whose denial pulls the chain of
  axioms underneath it into question too.

  Edit. If A already contains the chosen axiom -- matched either against the
  axiom's text or against its name appearing in the assumption -- mark that
  assumption negated and load_bearing. Otherwise append the axiom to A as a
  new assumption with load_bearing true and negated true. E, Rel, T and G are
  untouched.

  Frame move. Negating the top axiom of the ranking is leaving the frame, so
  in that case, and only in that case, F also moves to its nearest neighbour
  in the disciplinary similarity matrix
  (assets/disciplinary_similarity.json). Downstream operators then read the
  new frame's axioms and sorts.

Worked example. F is euclidean-geometry, whose five axioms rank angle-sum
(depth 0), euclidean-metric (depth 0), pythagorean (depth 1),
triangle-inequality (depth 1), parallel-postulate (depth 2). At rho = 2.0,
rho_hat = 0.25 and i = floor(0.25 * 5) = 1: R' negates "the ambient space
carries the Euclidean metric". At rho = 8.0, rho_hat = 1.0 and i = 4: R'
negates "the parallel postulate holds" -- the most derived axiom, resting on
both the metric and the angle sum -- and because that is the last entry in
the ranking F also moves euclidean-geometry -> geometry, which is the
classical route out of the frame. The problem the search must still answer is
the original Euclidean one.

2. a line beginning "change log:" naming the axiom, its depth, and the frame
   move if one happened.
