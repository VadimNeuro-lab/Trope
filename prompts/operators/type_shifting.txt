Specification. Pick one entity e, then retype it and repair the relations
incident to it.

  Target entity. Sampled with probability proportional to degree(e) + 1. The
  paper says proportional to degree; the +1 is there because a degree-0
  entity would otherwise be unreachable, and a freshly parsed R usually has
  several.

  Target type tau. Take the entity role types other than T(e), ranked by the
  T_E similarity matrix in assets/type_similarity.json from most to least
  similar to T(e). Radicality indexes into that ranking:

    rho_hat = min(rho, rho_max) / rho_max,  rho_max is sampling.rho_max, default 8.0
    i       = min(n - 1, floor(rho_hat * n))   over the n ranked candidates

  so a small rho moves the entity to its nearest neighbour in type space and
  a large rho moves it as far as the vocabulary allows. Set T(e) = tau.

  Relation repair. Relations incident to e are retyped where the rewrite is
  unambiguous: depends_on becomes derived_from when e becomes an output at
  the source end, derived_from becomes depends_on when e becomes an input or
  a parameter, depends_on becomes indexes when e becomes a coordinate, and
  constrains becomes bounds when e becomes an output at the destination end.
  Any relation matching none of these keeps its type.

  Sort shift. When rho_hat > 0.5 the entity's "sort" also moves to a sibling
  sort -- another sort under the same supertype in the sort lattice of
  assets/type_similarity.json. This is the part of the edit the schema cannot
  see: both sorts validate, and the semantics change anyway.

Worked example. R holds the entity budget with type "parameter", sort
"memory_bound" and degree 3, and the relation depends_on(budget, plan). The
ranking from "parameter" is variable, constant, input, coordinate, output. At
rho = 6.0, rho_hat = 0.75 and i = floor(0.75 * 5) = 3, so tau = "coordinate":
the fixed resource budget becomes an axis the problem is indexed along.
depends_on(budget, plan) is repaired to indexes(budget, plan). Since
rho_hat > 0.5 the sort moves within numeric_bound, memory_bound to
wall_clock, so R' now describes a family of plans indexed by a time budget.
